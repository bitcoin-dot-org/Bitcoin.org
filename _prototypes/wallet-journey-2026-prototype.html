A wallet is the app that keeps the keys to your bitcoin. Whoever holds the keys, holds the coins — so this choice matters. We'll walk you through it.

Bitcoin doesn't live inside the wallet. It lives on the Bitcoin network. Your wallet holds the keys that prove the bitcoin is yours — like the only key to a house.

That's why the choice matters: lose the keys, lose the bitcoin. Let someone else hold them, and you're trusting them completely. The right wallet depends on how much you'll keep and how hands-on you want to be — which is exactly what we'll ask you.
