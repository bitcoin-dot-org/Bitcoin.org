Understanding the number

There is no single official Bitcoin price

Bitcoin trades around the world on many independent markets. Each market has its own buyers, sellers, liquidity, and local currency, so quoted prices can differ slightly from one service to another.

This page uses an aggregated market price to provide a broad reference. It is not a price at which bitcoin.org offers to buy or sell bitcoin.

Why does the price move?

  • Changes in global supply and demand
  • Market liquidity and trading activity
  • News, adoption, and economic conditions
  • Differences between currencies and exchanges
